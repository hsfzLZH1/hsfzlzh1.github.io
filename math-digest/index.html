How to read the ratings

Importance measures relative mathematical significance across the collection: 1 is a focused contribution, 2 a useful specialized advance, 3 a substantial advance within a field, 4 a major advance with broad consequences or methods, and 5 an exceptional advance on a central problem. Ratings use the full scale as coverage grows and may be recalibrated. Each digest explains its score.

An importance rating assesses the significance of the stated result if correct. A completed digest means its exposition is ready to read; it does not indicate expert verification.

Sources
